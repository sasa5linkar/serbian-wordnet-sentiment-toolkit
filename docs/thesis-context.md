# Dissertation context

The toolkit derives from research for Saša Z. Petalinkar’s doctoral
dissertation “Машинско учење и велики језички модели у развоју семантичких
мрежа и њиховој примени на аутоматско разумевање текста”, University of
Belgrade, 2026.

The dissertation has been submitted and awaits publication. This software repository
does not claim a DOI, defense date, or permanent thesis URL. Official metadata
will be added after publication.
